Recap · Why AURA exists

Campus problems get reported. Then they disappear.

Today

No tracking

WhatsApp groups, a paper box and flat Google Forms. Students never learn if anything happened.

Today

No priority

The loudest complaint wins, not the one affecting the most students.

Today

Fear of reprisal

A named complaint about a lab, hostel or office can follow a student. So it goes unreported.

AURA

5-state lifecycle

PENDING → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED / REJECTED, with a public resolution note and an audit trail.

AURA

Hype engine

One upvote per verified student pushes the most widespread problems to the top of Trending.

AURA

Anonymity vault

Reports carry no author. Only the student who filed it can see their own receipt.

Impact of engineering on society PO6

Who it helps, what could go wrong, how we'd know

Benefit

Voice without risk

  • Students report safety, hygiene and lab issues without fear
  • Admins get a ranked queue, not a WhatsApp scroll
  • Public resolution notes build institutional trust (SDG 16)
  • Fewer lost lab hours from broken equipment (SDG 4)
Risk & mitigation

Anonymity can be abused

  • Spam / false reports → verified @tkmce.ac.in accounts only, one vote each
  • Pile-ons → admin can REJECT with a public reason
  • Unequal access → works on any phone browser, no app install
How to evaluate

Metrics already recorded

  • Median time PENDING → RESOLVED (from submission_history)
  • % resolved vs rejected per category
  • Repeat reports on the same location

Ethics and inclusivity PO7

Ethical issues we hit, and what we did

Privacy vs accountability

Chose structural anonymity (no author column) over "trust the admin". Abuse is checked by verified accounts and one vote per student, not by identifying people.

Collect only what's needed

Passwords stored only as BCrypt hashes. CSV export carries no identity (tested). Matches the purpose-limitation principle of India's DPDP Act 2023.

Never mislead users

No sample or fake data: every screen shows the live database. The UI never shows success unless the database confirmed it.

Inclusive by default

WCAG AA contrast, light + dark themes, phone-first layout, free to use. Known gap: staff without a @tkmce.ac.in email can't report yet.

Secrets never committed · service-role key never sent to the browserdocs/SECURITY.md · AGENT.md

Adaptability and self-learning PO11

What we taught ourselves, and when we changed course

Learned beyond the syllabus
  • Cloud PostgreSQL over JDBC, connection management
  • Postgres grants and SECURITY DEFINER functions
  • OAuth 2.0 PKCE (RFC 8252) and JWT sessions
  • Browser end-to-end testing with Playwright
  • CI/CD with GitHub Actions; PWAs and service workers
Times we changed course
  • In-memory → live DB (6 → 11 Sep): the prototype service was replaced by real DAOs once the schema stabilised
  • Google OAuth → email/password: a desktop JAR cannot keep an OAuth client secret secret
  • Local-storage web demo → real RPCs: the portal must never show success that the DB did not confirm
