FAQ

A few things to know.

Can administrators see who filed a report?

No. Reports have no author field. Your ownership link is stored separately in a private receipt that only your signed-in account can read.

Who can sign in?

Students and staff with a college-issued @tkmce.ac.in account approved for AURA. Your role is assigned by the college.

What happens after I submit a report?

Your ticket appears as Pending. Admins review, assign and update it, then publish a resolution note—or a reason if it can't be acted on.

I can't sign in. What should I do?

Accounts are created by the college, so there's no public sign-up. Ask the AURA administrators to approve your @tkmce.ac.in email, then confirm it from your inbox.
